Standardized Behavioral Anchors

Qualitative guidelines for rating voice performance across 1, 3, and 5 scores.

Filter Dimension:

Consistency

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Persona or voice tone noticeably shifts, fluctuates, or breaks persona across turns.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Mostly stable voice identity with occasional subtle tonality shifts.

5 - Strong Anchor

Rock-solid, consistent voice identity, pitch, and accent throughout entire session.

Fluency

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Fragmented, robotic, or disjointed speech; unnatural phrase boundaries.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Clear and understandable, though slightly artificial phrasing is noticeable.

5 - Strong Anchor

Smooth, natural, human-like cadence and phrasing without awkward syntax.

Latency

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Long delay (>2.5s) disrupts conversational flow and user confidence.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Noticeable turn-taking delay (1.2s–2.5s) but within acceptable bounds.

5 - Strong Anchor

Crisp, prompt responses (<1.2s) matching human conversational speed.

Turn-taking

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Talks over user or fails to yield turn; awkward endpoint detection.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Generally effective yielding with occasional premature interruptions.

5 - Strong Anchor

Smooth natural listening, yields seamlessly, closes turns cleanly.

Pronunciation

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Mispronounces key domain terms, customer names, or common words.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Minor pronunciation slips that do not impede user comprehension.

5 - Strong Anchor

Flawless, crisp pronunciation of specialized lexicon and names.

Pacing

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Uncomfortably fast, sluggishly slow, or poorly segmented delivery.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Generally clear pace; occasional uneven speed during long responses.

5 - Strong Anchor

Pacing dynamically matches conversation complexity and user needs.

Prosody

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Flat, robotic monotone or exaggerated, context-inappropriate emphasis.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Adequate pitch inflection, though somewhat synthetic on long turns.

5 - Strong Anchor

Rich, natural pitch variations and stress patterns reinforcing meaning.

Dead air

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Unexplained long silence (>3s) with no audio filler or progress signal.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Brief pauses before tool/API calls; tolerable but slightly jarring.

5 - Strong Anchor

Clean pauses; uses natural thinking fillers or filler audio during latencies.

Interruption handling

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Ignores user interjections, continues speaking, or loses context state.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Stops speaking upon user interruption but recovers state with minor friction.

5 - Strong Anchor

Instantly halts speech, preserves state context, and smoothly yields.

Confidence

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Sounds hesitant, overly apologetic, or contradicts previous statements.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Delivers accurate info with slightly tentative tone during complex flows.

5 - Strong Anchor

Clear, authoritative, and confident delivery across all turns.

Trust

1-5 Evaluation Standard
1 - Poor Anchor

Hallucinates facts, conceals uncertainty, or gives misleading info.

3 - Acceptable Anchor

Safe and truthful; occasionally inconsistent in transparency during errors.

5 - Strong Anchor

Highly transparent, accurate, gracefully handles uncertainty or handoffs.
